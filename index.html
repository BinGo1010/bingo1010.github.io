01 / ABOUT

About Me

I am currently pursuing a master's degree in Mechanical Engineering at South China University of Technology. My research experience bridges mechanical design, intelligent control, rehabilitation robotics, and multimodal physiological sensing.

I have contributed to projects ranging from robot mechanism design and automated drilling-rig equipment to EEG–fNIRS systems and hip-assist exoskeletons. I am particularly interested in taking an idea through the full development cycle—from design and validation to small-batch production and commercialization.
